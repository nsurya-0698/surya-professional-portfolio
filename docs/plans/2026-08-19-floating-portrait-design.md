# Floating Portrait Design

## Goal

Replace the framed hero portrait with a more authentic presentation that keeps Surya's photo visually connected to the cinematic portfolio without resembling a profile card.

## Approved treatment

- Extract the existing portrait onto a transparent background without changing identity, clothing, pose, expression, or crop.
- Remove the shaped frame, border, inset background, and orbit rings.
- Present the portrait as a free-standing cutout over a soft cyan-violet ambient glow.
- Preserve the two compact professional context labels.
- Animate the portrait with a restrained vertical float and a slowly breathing glow.
- Disable the motion when the visitor prefers reduced motion.

## Validation

- Confirm the generated asset has an alpha channel.
- Verify desktop and mobile hero composition.
- Run lint and a production build.
- Keep the work local for user review; do not push.
