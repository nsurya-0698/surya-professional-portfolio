# Recruiter-First Portfolio Refresh

## Goal

Reposition the existing portfolio for AI platform, cloud, and backend software engineering roles while preserving its established dark visual identity. Remove all claims about total years of experience and align public content with the verified master resume.

## Content direction

- Lead with AI platform and backend engineering rather than a broad full-stack label.
- Present Oracle work through AGS, WebSearch, deployment readiness, testing, observability, security, and operational reliability.
- Correct job titles, dates, contact links, metadata, and the downloadable resume.
- Replace arbitrary skill percentages with grouped, evidence-backed capabilities.
- Prioritize completed or defensible projects and case studies; remove unfinished concepts that weaken the portfolio.
- Keep appreciation content only when it can be represented as attributable professional feedback.

## Experience and interaction

- Preserve the existing color palette, typography, photograph, responsive navigation, and timeline identity.
- Remove background music and automatic project rotation to reduce distraction.
- Make projects directly browsable in a stable responsive grid.
- Keep clear recruiter actions: view experience, inspect projects, download resume, visit GitHub or LinkedIn, and make contact.

## Quality requirements

- Responsive at desktop and mobile widths.
- Keyboard-accessible navigation and controls.
- Accurate page title, description, keywords, canonical URL, and social metadata.
- No broken internal assets or outdated resume links.
- Clean lint and production build.
- Local Chrome review for hierarchy, wrapping, navigation, and console errors.

## Implementation checklist

1. Update metadata, hero, contact links, and navigation.
2. Align the experience timeline with the master resume.
3. Rebuild projects as stable verified case-study cards.
4. Replace skill confidence meters with grouped capability cards.
5. Remove background music and update downloadable resume.
6. Validate lint, build, desktop, mobile, links, and console output.
